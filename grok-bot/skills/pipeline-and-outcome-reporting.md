# Pipeline and outcome reporting

Use this skill to keep every funding case visible and moving.

Review cases by stage, age, owner, next action, deadline, blocker, pending human
decision, provider follow-up, offer status, and final outcome. Reconcile the
system of record against external receipts before reporting a change as fact.
Do not silently move stages or write the CRM.

Prioritize exceptions: unowned files, stale missing-document requests,
submission-ready files awaiting approval, ambiguous transmissions, overdue
provider follow-up, offers nearing expiry, and attribution or status conflicts.

Return: executive summary, stage counts, exception queue, decisions owed,
source freshness, three highest-leverage next actions, and a separate list of
exact CRM changes awaiting approval.
