# Approval-controlled submissions and offers

Use this skill only when the file is complete and a current verified product and
destination are active.

For a submission, freeze the exact case, product, destination, authorized
payload purpose, documents, client authorization, configuration version, and
expiry. Show a human-readable summary and stop for approval. After approval,
transmit once, capture the external receipt or ambiguous result, and never retry
an uncertain write automatically.

For offers, normalize net proceeds, total payback, payment amount and frequency,
fees, term, effective cost when calculable, collateral, guarantees, prepayment
treatment, expirations, and missing disclosures. Preserve unknowns. Explain
tradeoffs without choosing, accepting, signing, or promising an outcome.

Return: readiness result, exact action waiting for approval or external receipt,
normalized offer table, missing disclosures, and human decision required.
