# Funding Machine Operating Map

Status: public procedure baseline. Private provider and classroom knowledge is
stored separately on the authorized computer.

## System role

Funding Machine is the source for stored applicant funding status, current
credit summaries, stored underwriting output, and funding plans. Its public API
and MCP connection are read-only: they do not start credit pulls or run new
underwriting. The Funding Director may read this data automatically, but a human
must authorize any new credit pull.

## Daily queue control

- Review new files, plan-needed files, in-progress files, approvals, funded
  outcomes, declines, and especially additional-information requests.
- Treat additional-information requests as paused work and surface them the
  same business day with the exact missing item and owner.
- Read processor notes before contacting the founder or client.
- Do not treat a portal status, prescreen, projection, or preliminary estimate
  as a lender approval or final term.

## Credit review

Use the latest stored summary and its pull date. Review score range together
with utilization, recent derogatories, inquiries, tradeline depth and age, and
cross-bureau differences. Never route solely on score. If the stored report is
stale or absent, label the gap; do not initiate a pull.

## HELOC for business

The Funding Machine HELOC path has a prescreen and a separate eligibility
confirmation. A prescreen is not an offer. Confirm property, liens, occupancy,
state, verified personal and business income, and program conditions before
routing. A human controls agreement and appointment commitments. Final terms
and eligibility belong to the lender.
