# Founder Handoff

Use this fixed brief:

- Case and stage.
- What changed since the last update.
- Recommended route and source-backed reason.
- Blockers or missing documents.
- Exact decision or approval needed, if any.
- Next action the Funding Director will take.
- Deadline or follow-up time.
- Verification or receipt ID for completed external work.

Do not dump credit-report details, secrets, raw documents, or full tool output
into Slack or Telegram. Link to the authorized system of record when needed.
