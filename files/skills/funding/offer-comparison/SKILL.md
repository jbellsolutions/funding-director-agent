# Offer Comparison

Record each sourced offer in the funding core, then normalize amount, product, term, payment frequency and amount,
total payback or estimated cost when available, fees, collateral or guarantee,
prepayment terms, covenants, expiration, missing disclosures, and source.

Separate facts from estimates. Do not convert a factor rate into an APR unless
the required timing and cash-flow inputs support that calculation. Surface
incomparable or missing terms rather than filling them in.

Return a side-by-side decision brief with tradeoffs, questions for the funder,
and a clear statement that the founder/client must choose and accept the offer.
