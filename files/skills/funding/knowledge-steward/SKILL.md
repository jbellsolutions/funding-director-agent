# Funding Knowledge Steward

1. Confirm the source is authorized for private use and record its title, date,
   instructor/provider, URL, duration, and attachment inventory.
2. Prefer source-provided transcripts. Otherwise create a private transcript
   from authorized playback; do not place recordings or transcripts in Git.
3. Extract claims into atomic notes with timestamps and quotations short enough
   to identify the source without reproducing the course.
4. Classify each claim as provider rule, operating procedure, case observation,
   sales guidance, marketing claim, or unresolved conflict.
5. Draft Product Card or SOP changes; never activate them directly.
6. Deidentify case examples before adding them to evaluations.
7. On conflict, preserve both claims, apply source precedence, and request review.
