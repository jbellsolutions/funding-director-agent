# Product Routing

1. Run the deterministic product recommendation tool for the case.
2. Exclude every inactive, expired, paused, or blocked Product Card from a
   customer-facing recommendation.
3. Rank up to three active routes by fit, sequencing, document readiness,
   downside, and the client's purpose—not by commission.
4. Show why each route fits, why it may fail, documents still needed, timing
   range, cost structure if verified, and the evidence/effective date.
5. If no active route qualifies, return `no_verified_active_route`; recommend
   nurture, missing-information work, or human review rather than guessing.
6. Public marketing claims may identify a family to investigate but never
   establish an amount, approval probability, price, or submit-ready route.
