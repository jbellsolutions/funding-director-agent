# Funding Options Document Builder

Read the case, verified Product Cards, recorded offers, desired use of funds,
timing, material risks, disclosures, and next step. Mark anything missing as
`unknown`; never invent approval odds, provider terms, savings, or rates.

Build in this order:

1. One-page case summary.
2. Stated funding objective and timing.
3. Eligible product families and why, sourced to verified Product Cards.
4. Recorded offers in a like-for-like comparison table.
5. Known total payback, fees, payment frequency, estimated monthly debt service,
   prepayment terms, collateral, and guarantees.
6. Missing or unverified terms.
7. Risks, assumptions, disclosures, expiry, and the human decision required.

Create a private Markdown source first, then DOCX or PDF when useful. This skill
may draft but does not accept an offer, sign, send, or request a signature.
Before any approved delivery, show the exact recipient, filename, terms, expiry,
and message. Record the destination receipt after delivery and reconcile before
retrying an ambiguous action.
